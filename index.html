About

Alam Sher is an Agricultural Engineering graduate from the University of Agriculture Faisalabad. He is passionate about advancing modern agricultural practices through engineering solutions that improve crop productivity, optimize irrigation systems, and promote sustainable farming technologies.

His academic background and practical exposure focus on improving agricultural efficiency using engineering principles, water management strategies, and modern agricultural machinery.

Professional Projects & Research

High Efficiency Irrigation Systems (HEIS) Projects

Designed and supported implementation of HEIS irrigation systems including drip and sprinkler networks. Performed hydraulic calculations, system layout planning, and ensured compliance with client and government standards.

Irrigation System Installation & Commissioning

Supervised installation of irrigation systems including drip and sprinkler setups. Conducted system testing, pressure checks, and performance evaluations to ensure optimal water distribution and efficiency.

Farm Management & Irrigation Optimization

Managed irrigation scheduling, crop planning, and soil fertility strategies in R&D farm environments. Improved water usage efficiency through data-driven irrigation practices and soil analysis.

Soil & Water Management Research

Conducted experiments on soil-water relationships, irrigation efficiency, and crop growth. Collected and analyzed field data to optimize irrigation strategies and improve yield outcomes.

Irrigation Design & BOQ/BOM Preparation

Prepared irrigation layouts, bill of quantities (BOQ), and material selection for large-scale agricultural and landscape projects. Coordinated procurement and ensured project alignment with technical requirements.

MSc Research: Water Productivity Using DSSAT Model

Researched water productivity under deficit irrigation conditions using DSSAT modeling. Focused on optimizing water usage for wheat crops while maintaining maximum yield.
